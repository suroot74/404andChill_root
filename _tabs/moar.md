So, you’ve made it to the “Moar” section. Congratulations? This is where I’ve stashed a handful of silly little experiments - fun, pointless, and just the right kind of weird to fill a few minutes of your day. Think of it as the digital equivalent of those novelty toys you couldn’t resist buying from a gas station. Dive in if you dare. Or don’t. Whatever.

<a href="/coins" target="_blank">Roys Payphone Scramble →</a>  
Because nothing says "relaxation" like the anxiety of scrambling for loose change to make a call. Test your reflexes in this chaotic, mildly absurd game. Bonus points if you can hear the coins clinking in your head. Inspired by <a href="https://phonelosers.fandom.com/wiki/RBCP" target="_blank">Roy Gerbil</a>.

<a href="/payphone" target="_blank">Payphone Simulator →</a>  
Want to relive the tactile joy of clunking coins into a payphone and dialing a number that may or may not connect? No? Well, here it is anyway. Inspired by <a href="https://phonelosers.com/" target="_blank">The Phone Losers of America</a>. Sound clips taken from the <a href="https://phonelosers.com/media/torrents/pla_media_pack_2017.torrent">2017 PLA Media Pack (torrent)</a>.

<a href="/please-hold" target="_blank">The Infinite Hold Simulator →</a>  
Experience the thrill of being stuck on hold forever. It’s everything you hated about the ’90s, now conveniently online and endless. You’re welcome.

<a href="/retro-tech-quiz" target="_blank">Retro Tech Quiz →</a>  
Think you know your Betamax from your 8-track? Take this quiz to test your retro tech expertise and discover just how much useless knowledge you’ve retained over the years.

<a href="/static-tv" target="_blank">The TV Static Simulator →</a>  
Who doesn’t miss staring at a screen of grainy white noise, wondering if aliens are about to communicate? Now you can relive that weirdly mesmerizing experience anytime.

<a href="/vhs-simulator" target="_blank">Authentic VHS Simulator →</a>  
Remember the magic of tracking lines and fuzzy frames? No? Well, here’s a chance to experience it anyway. It’s as authentic as your nostalgia demands.
